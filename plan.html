Ring PRO US conversion plan

The homepage asks people to admire a $479 ring without telling them what it costs, what’s included, how sizing works or that HSA/FSA is an option. This plan fixes that and rebuilds HSA/FSA around the IRS guidance published on Sept 23. It comes with five redesigned pages, built on the live site’s design.

01

Three shifts

Each one changes what we build, not just what we say.

From admire → decide

Answer cost, inclusion and fit on the first screen

“Health looks good on you.”, $479 with the case included, “no membership required”, free sizing kit, 30-day returns. Today’s hero says “Power Moves” and shows no price.

From badge → route

Make HSA/FSA a guided payment route

It already pays for ~15% of US Ring PRO orders. After IRS Q&A-15, the win is a clear, compliant path to pay and document, not a bigger savings claim.

From promise → certainty

Show dates, terms and what happens next

Long ETAs caused 62% of launch-cohort full refunds. Delivery dates per fit path, cancellation terms and a dated US-availability answer go on the page.

03

HSA/FSA: card first

Ring PRO is SIGIS-listed, so HSA/FSA cards approve it at checkout. That makes the card the headline. The IRS rule only shapes the fine print.

Verified on irs.gov · Q15, added Sept 23 2026
A wearable “is a medical expense if the individual purchases and uses the device primarily to assist in the diagnosis, cure, mitigation, treatment, or prevention of a specific disease … A generalized desire to monitor or improve health is not sufficient.” Accessories and software subscriptions follow the same test.

Ring PRO has SIGIS approval (confirmed by UH, Oct 23)

Most HSA/FSA cards approve it automatically, with no letter. So the copy leads with the true action, “Pay with your HSA/FSA card”, everywhere: nav, hero, buy page, FAQ. The clinician letter is now only a fallback for declines. One caveat: card approval isn’t a tax determination under Q15, so a short “keep your receipt” note stays.

Still change this week

The live FAQ names Ring AIR, which can’t be sold in the US, and tells Shop Pay users to check out as guest. Avoid “~$335 after tax savings” and “up to” savings claims, which Flex and Truemed prohibit. Law-firm commentary says SIGIS may revisit dual-use wearables after Q15, so someone should own watching the SIGIS list.

HSA/FSA orders peak in January, not December

US web ring orders paid via Flex, Sika or TrueMed, Ring AIR era. In Nov–Dec HSA/FSA’s share drops to ~11% because Black Friday card volume swamps it, yet January sets the record. One season only. Treat it as a hypothesis to test, not a law.

05

Debate with Codex

Two rounds with gpt-6-astra. It made the plan more conservative and more operational. On one point it was wrong, and we checked.

Codex changed the plan

Adopted

  • Headline first, economics right under it; no “effective price”.
  • HSA/FSA as a hero link, not a tax-adjusted price. With SIGIS confirmed afterwards, the link now reads “Pay with your HSA/FSA card”.
  • No condition menus as savings routes; UH’s own terms describe a wellness product.
  • Delivery and cancellation certainty is P0; the current FAQ restricts cancellation after the kit ships.
  • One bundled 50/50 journey test. Our original 45–50k sessions per arm was too low.
  • Gift flow where the recipient sizes; one consolidated upgrades panel.
  • Plan-specific deadlines; HSA vs FSA responsibility stated separately.
We held

Agreed after round 2

  • HSA/FSA in the top nav and hero; it is ~15% of orders.
  • Compact cost-over-time module on the homepage, with named, dated sources.
  • Pay-in-4 stated as “4 interest-free payments of $119.75”, replacing the live “$120/mo”.
  • Free standard shipping inside the bundled arm, costed in contribution.
  • An evidence layer on every mock, toggled with “Show evidence & dependencies”.
Resolved by checking

Where Codex was wrong

  • Round 2 said Q&A-15 couldn’t be verified and asked us to drop the IRS quote. We fetched irs.gov: Q15 is there, added Sept 23 2026, and the quote matches word for word. The quote stays.
  • We kept the partial-balance split tool, gated on Flex confirmation, instead of removing it.
  • Codex insisted “Free return shipping” needs confirming. It’s in the live Ring PRO FAQ; Legal should re-confirm before launch.

The homepage gets ~8.6k US sessions a month. Ten separate A/B tests won’t reach significance before the holidays, so the plan is one journey test.

  1. 1
    Ship the fixes now, untested

    Remove misleading copy (“always eligible”, “$120/mo”, the stale “Pre-order now” banner, Ring AIR dead ends) and fix the cookie panel and chat overlap on mobile.

    NOW
  2. 2
    One 50/50 purchase-journey test

    Control: current homepage + buy page. Variant: new hero, nav, included-vs-optional table, fit paths with dates, consolidated upgrades, free standard shipping. Assign per visitor and keep pricing identical in both arms. If BFCM forces an offer change, stop and call it.

  3. 3

    Randomized expert invitations, sizing-kit follow-up reminders, the gift route for gift-intent traffic, and message-matched creator and “Oura alternative” traffic routed 50/50.

    GATE
  2. Flex
    Written answers

    Single-item partial balance, Shop Pay “Pay another way”, decline handling and clinician fallback fee. SIGIS status is confirmed; monitor it monthly.

    GATE
  3. Ops
    Policies the pages depend on

    Cancellation before size confirmation, live ETA per finish and fit path, free standard shipping, holiday returns to Jan 31, unredeemed-gift refunds, competitor-ring trade-in credit.

    DECIDE
  4. CX
    Verified Ring PRO reviews

    Post-delivery collection with dates, counts, critical reviews and responses. Trustpilot is 2.6/5 at company level, and fixing support matters more than any badge.
