Sizing section
— 10 lighter ideas

Same three facts (order today, ships by Oct 30, arrives Nov 2) and the same two paths (I know my size / send the free kit) — shown with far less weight.

What feels like too much

Three equal full-height photo cards pull as much attention as the hero. Photos here don’t explain sizing at all.

Repeated labels

Every step says it three times: “TODAY / Order / Finish and size”. Plus a “1” badge on all three steps.

Wrong heading

“PRO beyond measure” is the carousel’s heading, repeated. This section should talk about fit and delivery.

Dead toggle

“I know my size / Send the free kit” looks like a switch but changes nothing. It should drive the content (idea 04, 10).
