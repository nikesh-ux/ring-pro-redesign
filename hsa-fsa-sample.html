FAQ

Fair questions.

Can I use my HSA or FSA card?

Yes. Ring PRO is SIGIS-listed, so most cards approve it at checkout. Choose Flex and pay.

Do I need a doctor’s letter?

Usually not. If your plan asks, Flex can connect you to a clinician, who decides.

Card declined?

Check the message or your card administrator. Or pay another way and claim later.

Can I split the payment?

Yes. Eligible items on HSA/FSA, the rest on a regular card. One order.

Do add-ons count?

Coverage and memberships generally don’t. Some PowerPlugs may, if you qualify.

When does my FSA expire?

Usually at plan-year end, often Dec 31. Some plans allow a grace period or carryover.

Does my HSA expire?

No. It rolls over.

New plan year. Full FSA amount?

Generally yes, from day one. HSA funds arrive as deposited.

Buying for someone else?

Generally only you, your spouse and dependents. Same rules apply.

Is this tax advice?

No. Your plan and your tax pro have the final word.
