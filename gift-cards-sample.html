FAQ

Fair questions.

Does it expire?

No. Proposed: gift cards never expire, in line with state rules.

What can they buy?

Ring PRO and anything else on ultrahuman.com/us. If the order costs more, they pay the difference.

Can I pay with HSA/FSA?

Gift cards can’t be bought with HSA/FSA. The recipient can use their own HSA/FSA card on top of the gift card.

Is there a physical card?

Digital only. Print the email if you want something to hand over.

Can I change the date?

Yes, any time before it sends. Find it in your order confirmation.
