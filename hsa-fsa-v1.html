    How it works

    Pay for Ring PRO with your HSA or FSA in three steps.

    Step 1

    Choose Flex at checkout

    At checkout, select “Flex | Pay with HSA/FSA”. If you are logged into Shop Pay, please select “checkout as guest”.

    Step 2

    Pay using your HSA/FSA card

    Enter your HSA/FSA card details to complete the purchase. If you don’t have your HSA/FSA card, you can enter a credit or debit card. Some items require you to confirm your eligibility through a brief questionnaire.

    Step 3

    Get an itemized receipt

    Flex will email you an itemized receipt. Save this in case your HSA/FSA administrator requires more information.

    FAQ

    Fair questions.

    Can I use my HSA or FSA card?

    Yes. Ring PRO is SIGIS-listed, so most cards approve it at checkout. Choose Flex and pay.

    Do I need a doctor’s letter?

    Usually not. If your plan asks, Flex can connect you to a clinician, who decides.

    Card declined?

    Check the message or ask your card administrator. Or pay another way and claim later.

    Can I split the payment?

    Yes. Eligible items on HSA/FSA, the rest on a regular card. One order.

    Do add-ons count?

    Coverage and memberships generally don’t. Some PowerPlugs may, if you qualify.

    When does my FSA expire?

    Usually at plan-year end, often Dec 31. Some plans allow a grace period or carryover.

    Does my HSA expire?

    No. It rolls over.

    New plan year. Full FSA amount?

    Generally yes, from day one. HSA funds arrive as deposited.

    Buying for someone else?

    Generally only you, your spouse and dependents. The same rules apply.

    Is this tax advice?

    No. Your plan and your tax pro have the final word.

    Ring PRO is on the SIGIS eligible product list, so most HSA/FSA cards approve it at checkout. Keep your itemized receipt; your plan may ask for it. Ultrahuman does not provide tax advice.
