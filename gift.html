    Gift questions

    Fair questions.

    Between sizes?

    The kit has every size, 5–14. They wear one 24 hours, overnight too. Torn? A specialist helps by chat or video.

    They already own one?

    Ring AIR sizes match, so they confirm instantly. Don’t want two? They decline and you’re refunded.

    HSA/FSA for a gift?

    HSA and FSA funds generally cover only you, your spouse and dependents. Eligibility depends on your circumstances, so a gift for anyone else usually won’t qualify. See the HSA/FSA page.

    Will they see what I paid?

    Not a cent. They see the gift, never the price. Refunds stay with you.

    Second thoughts on the message?

    Edit it or the date until the email sends or the box ships.

    What do they need?

    An iPhone or Android and the free Ultrahuman app. That’s it.
